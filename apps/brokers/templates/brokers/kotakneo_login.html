Getting the TOTP Secret (one-time setup)

  1. Go to Kotak Neo Developer Portal and log in
  2. Navigate to TOTP Registration
  3. A QR code is shown along with a base32 secret string (like JBSWY3DPEHPK3PXP)
  4. Optionally scan the QR with Google Authenticator to verify — but for our system, you only need the text secret
  5. Paste the base32 string in the TOTP Secret Key field above

This is a static key that never expires unless you re-register. The system uses it to generate time-based codes automatically — identical to what Google Authenticator would show, just without you having to look at your phone.
